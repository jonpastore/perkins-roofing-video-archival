GOTCHAS
- `pkill -f <pattern>` MATCHES YOUR OWN SHELL and kills it mid-command. Put the pattern in a FILE.
  (Documented already. Walked into it anyway.)
- VERIFY THE ARTIFACT, NOT THE SOURCE. Every article defect that escaped lived in the gap between
  content_md and published HTML. Read back what WordPress STORED (context=edit) — the CDN cache
  made one pass look clean when it wasn't.
- A regex that matches more than it means: `<a` also matches `<aside`, and with DOTALL `.*?</a>`
  swallows whole callout bodies. `(?![a-z])` is load-bearing. `<a id="x">` is an anchor TARGET.
- `terraform plan` RESOLVES DATA SOURCES, so it needs read access to any secret read that way;
  secretmanager.viewer is not enough. A write-only attribute (Identity Platform client_secret)
  can never be verified and plans forever — ignore_changes.

  token into 219 chars of prose. Use 2>/dev/null.
