## Frozen revision

Commercial and cancellation policy is an external, approved, effective-dated configuration. The
system never determines statutory applicability, interprets contract terms, or invents legal
periods. It records policy source/version and application evidence, and fails closed to
`POLICY_REVIEW_REQUIRED` on absent or ambiguous policy. Production configuration still requires
approved policy values/content (`EXT-GL-06`), but their absence is not a system-design defect.
